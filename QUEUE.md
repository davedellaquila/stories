# Stories — Release Queue

Dave's program (set Nov 15, 2026): develop every story on the outline, stockpile the
finished ones, and release ONE every Sunday as the featured weekend read about the
history of the area — published here in the Stories collection and featured in the
Fiddletown Gazette.

## How a release works (Sunday, ~7:00 AM PT)
1. Take the next story below whose status is READY (built and converted, not yet released).
2. Publish it: its folder goes live in this repo, a card is added at the top of
   `index.html`, its outline entry flips to Published with a "Read it" link, and
   README.md's story list gains it. Commit + push, then verify the served copy.
3. Send the Fiddletown Gazette desk the story's URL to feature as Sunday's weekend read.
4. Mark it RELEASED with the date, and report to Dave in the main chat — including
   how many READY stories remain in the stockpile. If none are READY, publish nothing
   and tell Dave the stockpile is empty.

Statuses: QUEUED (not built) · BUILDING · READY (stockpiled) · RELEASED (date).
