The Snowshoe Thompson Story

For twenty winters, 1856 to 1876, John "Snowshoe" Thompson carried the U.S. mail on ten-foot oak skis between Placerville and Genoa — up to a hundred pounds of mail, medicine, and Comstock news over the Sierra, through storms that stopped everyone else. He was never paid. The route, the rescues, and the man.
